### Confirmed content issues

1. The standalone policy's “only with service providers” sharing description
   does not cover the lender/funder sharing described in the application policy
   and authorization. Readers receive materially different explanations.
2. The standalone collection summary describes contact details and company
   information but does not explicitly identify the form's SSNs, dates of birth,
   bank statements, financial details, and signatures. The longer policy is
   closer, but it too should identify uploaded documents and signatures clearly.
3. The policies present different effective/update dates without explaining
   that they are different notices or identifying which version governs.
   Old dates alone are not proof of an error; inconsistent versions are the issue.
4. The link promises “Terms & Privacy,” but opens no separately identified Terms.
   The modal also refers to changing “these terms,” mixing policy and contract
   terminology without distinguishing them.
5. The application authorization identifies “Biz Bulker Inc dba No Limit Capital,”
   while the policies identify the brand and, in the modal, unnamed subsidiaries
   and affiliates. The responsible business identity should be made consistent
   after the legal identity is confirmed.
6. Only the standalone version includes the retention/request sections. A reader
   who sees only the application modal does not receive the same explanation.

### Operational claims that cannot be verified from source alone

- “We do not sell your personal information”: confirm actual business practices.
  The fact that application information may be shared does not, on its own,
  resolve whether that statement accurately describes those practices.
- Retention and access/correction/deletion requests: confirm the real process
  and responsible contact. The code's lack of an automatic deletion job does not
  establish that the business has no manual retention/deletion process.
- Device/browser information, cookies and analytics: no first-party analytics
  or cookie-setting code was found in the reviewed frontend. Hosting logs and
  third-party services may behave differently; confirm the actual production setup.
- Text messaging: the application requests SMS consent, but the policies do not
  clearly describe the channel and preference-handling process. Confirm that
  process before adding promises about it.

Before one policy is published in both places, confirm:

1. The approved legal business identity and policy text.
2. The actual recipients and purposes for sharing application information.
3. Whether the standalone “We do not sell your personal information” statement
   accurately describes the business's practices.
4. The retention process and how access/correction/deletion requests are handled.
5. The appropriate effective/update dates and whether separate Terms exist.

Privacy-policy substance, effective dates, consent wording, statistics,
testimonials, experience figures, and portal promises remain unchanged. Once
approved, both policy views
should use the same content and date, and the link label should identify exactly
what it opens.

General reference: the FTC's [consumer privacy guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/consumer-privacy)
emphasizes being clear about data practices and honoring privacy promises. This
comparison identifies source/content inconsistencies; it does not infer business
practices that have not been confirmed.
